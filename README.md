## 🕷️ My Favorite Movie: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* 🕸️

> "Anyone can wear the mask." — the movie's main message

*Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* is an animated film released in **2018** by **Sony Pictures Animation**. It follows ***Miles Morales***, a teenager from Brooklyn who becomes Spider-Man and meets other Spider-People from different dimensions.

### 🦸 Main Characters
- **Miles Morales**: the new Spider-Man from Brooklyn
- **Peter B. Parker**: an older, tired Spider-Man who mentors Miles
- **Gwen Stacy**: Spider-Woman from another dimension
- **Kingpin**: the villain who opens the portal between worlds
- **Other Spider-People**
  - Spider-Man Noir
  - Peni Parker
  - Spider-Ham
